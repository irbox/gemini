Our newest TPUs: 8tx and 8i Max, built for the agentic era

Meet Cloud TPU v8tx and v8i Max — custom-engineered silicon designed to power the next trillion-parameter frontier models and autonomous agent swarms.

Google Cloud Infrastructure Team
Silicon & Distributed Systems
November 10, 2026 • 5 min

As machine learning systems transition from static question-answering to persistent, long-running agentic swarms, data center architectures must evolve to match the compute and memory bandwidth demands.

Today, we're unveiling our custom 8th-generation Tensor Processing Units: Cloud TPU v8tx for extreme frontier pre-training and TPU v8i Max for low-latency agent inference.

Architectural Innovations

  • Optical Circuit Switching (OCS): Dynamic topology reconfiguration connecting up to 65,536 chips in a single pod without packet drops.
  • Next-generation HBM4 Memory: 4.8 TB/s memory bandwidth per chip, keeping ultra-dense MoE models hot and responsive.
  • Liquid Cooling Efficiency: 100% direct-to-chip liquid cooling designed to operate at industry-leading Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE < 1.08).
