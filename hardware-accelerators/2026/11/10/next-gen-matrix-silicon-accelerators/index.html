Next-Gen Matrix Silicon: Custom Microarchitectures for Agentic Computing

Meet Matrix-8X and Matrix-8i: Custom-engineered 3nm silicon designed to power trillion-parameter models with direct-to-chip microfluidic cooling.

kai-chen
2026-11-10 10:30:00 -0800 • 6 min read

As machine learning systems transition from static single-turn query responses to continuous, persistent swarms of autonomous agents, datacenter infrastructure must undergo a fundamental microarchitectural shift.

Today, we are lifting the hood on our custom Matrix-8X training accelerator and Matrix-8i low-latency inference silicon.

Microarchitectural Specifications

  • Optical Circuit Switching (OCS): Dynamic optical routing directly interlinks up to 65,536 accelerator nodes without packet drops, reducing inter-pod communication overhead by 73%.
  • HBM4 Ultra-Bandwidth Interconnects: Delivering 4.8 TB/s of dedicated memory bandwidth per die to keep massive weights hot.
  • Direct-to-Chip Microfluidic Channels: Custom etched microchannels allow dielectric coolant to circulate within microns of the transistor junctions, achieving a datacenter PUE of 1.06.
