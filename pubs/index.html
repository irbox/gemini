NeurIPS 2017 (Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems) 2017 Natural Language Processing

Attention Is All You Need

Authors: Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, Illia Polosukhin

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks in an encoder-decoder configuration. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely. Experiments on two machine translation tasks show these models to be superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train.

Nature 2024 / Physical Review X 2024 Quantum AI

Quantum Error Correction Below the Surface Code Threshold with Willow

Authors: Google Quantum AI, Hartmut Neven, Julian Kelly, Anthony Megrant, Pedram Roushan

Building a fault-tolerant quantum computer requires physical error rates below the threshold where increasing code distance exponentially reduces logical error rates. Here we demonstrate a 105-qubit superconducting quantum processor named Willow, achieving distance-3, distance-5, and distance-7 surface code memory below the fault-tolerance threshold, halving the logical error rate with each step in code size.

Nature 2024 2024 Science AI

Accurate Structure Prediction of Biomolecular Interactions with AlphaFold 3

Authors: Josh Abramson, Jonas Adler, Jack Dunger, Richard Evans, Tim Green, Alexander Pritzel, Olaf Ronneberger, John Jumper, Demis Hassabis

Here we introduce AlphaFold 3, with a substantially updated diffusion-based architecture capable of predicting the joint structure of complexes including proteins, nucleic acids (DNA and RNA), small molecules, ions, and chemical modifications. AlphaFold 3 achieves unprecedented accuracy across diverse biomolecular interactions, substantially improving protein-ligand binding pose prediction compared with traditional docking tools.

Google DeepMind Technical Report / arXiv:2312.11805 2023 Machine Intelligence

Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models

Authors: Gemini Team, Jeff Dean, Demis Hassabis, Oriol Vinyals, Koray Kavukcuoglu

We present Gemini, a new family of highly capable multimodal models trained jointly across image, audio, video, and text modalities. Gemini Ultra achieves state-of-the-art results on 30 of 32 widely-used academic benchmarks, including being the first model to outperform human experts on MMLU (Massive Multitask Language Understanding) with a score of 90.04%.

Science 2023 2023 Earth AI

Learning Skillful Medium-Range Global Weather Forecasting with GraphCast

Authors: Remi Lam, Alvaro Sanchez-Gonzalez, Matthew Willson, Peter Wirnsberger, Meire Fortunato, Ferran Alet, Suman Ravuri, Alexander Pritzel

Global medium-range weather forecasting is critical to decision-making across society. We introduce GraphCast, a machine learning-based weather forecasting system that generates 10-day global forecasts at 0.25-degree resolution in under one minute on a single TPU. GraphCast outperforms the world's premier operational deterministic system on 90.3% of 1,380 verification targets.

arXiv preprint arXiv:2404.18416 2024 Health AI

Capabilities of Gemini Models in Medicine

Authors: Khaled Saab, Tao Tu, Wei-Hung Weng, Ryutaro Tanno, David Stutz, Ellery Wulczyn, Yun Liu, Alan Karthikesalingam, Vivek Natarajan

Generalist multimodal AI models have the potential to assist clinicians across diverse diagnostic and operational tasks. We present Med-Gemini, a family of multimodal models fine-tuned and equipped with web-search retrieval and long-context capabilities for clinical medicine. Med-Gemini achieves 91.1% on MedQA (USMLE), outperforming previous state-of-the-art models while demonstrating physician-level performance in radiology report generation and clinical summarization.

NAACL-HLT 2019 2019 Natural Language Processing

BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding

Authors: Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang, Kenton Lee, Kristina Toutanova

We introduce a new language representation model called BERT, which stands for Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. Unlike recent language representation models, BERT is designed to pre-train deep bidirectional representations from unlabeled text by jointly conditioning on both left and right context in all layers. As a result, the pre-trained BERT model can be fine-tuned with just one additional output layer to create state-of-the-art models for a wide range of tasks.

ISCA 2023 (ACM/IEEE International Symposium on Computer Architecture) 2023 Software & Hardware Systems

TPU v4: An Optically Reconfigurable Supercomputer for Machine Learning with Hardware Support for Embeddings

Authors: Norm Jouppi, George Kurian, Sheng Li, Peter Ma, Rahul Nagarajan, Llifeng Nai, Nishant Patil, Amin Vahdat, David Patterson

We describe Google's TPU v4 supercomputer, which integrates 4,096 chips interconnected via custom optical circuit switches (OCS) to dynamically configure topologies. TPU v4 supercomputers provide 10x speedup and 2-3x better energy efficiency compared to contemporary contemporary accelerators, powering Google's most demanding training workloads including PaLM and Gemini.
