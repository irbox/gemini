Welcome to **The Keyword**, Google's official blog.

Here you will find stories from our product managers, researchers, engineers, and leadership about the latest innovations in AI, cloud infrastructure, consumer hardware, open-source software, and environmental sustainability.

### What We Cover
- **Innovation & AI**: Frontier models from Google DeepMind, quantum computing breakthroughs, and developer tools.
- **Products & Platforms**: Deep dives into Android, Pixel phones, Chrome, Search, and Google Workspace features.
- **Technology & Infrastructure**: TPU accelerators, planetary optical networking, and datacenter engineering.
- **Company News & Culture**: Inside looks at our culture, our people, and global outreach initiatives.
