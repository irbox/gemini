Research Focus Areas

The Lovelace Lab conducts open, foundational computing research spanning algorithmic theory, custom matrix silicon, fault-tolerant quantum error correction, and bio-cybernetic interfaces.

Research Pillar

Applied AI & Sciences

Leveraging foundational computational methods to accelerate scientific breakthroughs, from drug discovery and genomics to atmospheric and climate modeling.

Health AI & Bio-Signals

Non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring, clinical assistant models, multimodal diagnostic telemetry.

Science AI & Molecular Dynamics

Neural quantum chemistry, crystal structure generation, protein binding simulations.

Climate, Earth & Sustainability

Real-time flood inundation mapping, micro-weather forecasting, renewable grid balancing.

Research Pillar

Foundational ML & Reasoning

Pioneering next-generation cognitive architectures, test-time compute trajectory planning, and neural-symbolic theorem proving.

Machine Intelligence & Search

Monte Carlo tree search in token spaces, self-verifying inference loops, long-context retrieval.

Perception & Multimodal Grounding

High-resolution diagram vectorization, 3D world representation, zero-copy in-browser shaders.

Algorithms & Optimization

Sublinear-time graph streaming algorithms, combinatorial optimization, formal compiler verification.

Research Pillar

People, Systems & Quantum AI

Constructing the physical and algorithmic substrates of future computation—from distance-7 topological surface codes to energy-proportional custom matrix silicon.

Quantum Hardware & Surface Codes

Cryogenic CMOS interfaces, sub-microsecond syndromic error decoding, logical qubit scaling.

Custom Silicon & Photonic Fabrics

Systolic matrix execution arrays, near-memory SRAM compute, on-interposer optical micro-ring routing.

Autonomous Robotics & SLAM

Asynchronous multi-agent consensus, visual-inertial odometry under extreme radiation and light.

Responsible AI & Algorithmic Ethics

Mechanistic interpretability, watermarking, safety constraints, formal algorithmic guarantees.
