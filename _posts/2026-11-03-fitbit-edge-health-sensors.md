Today, **Fitbit Edge** is officially available online and at retailers worldwide.

Fitbit Edge is engineered to help you gain deeper insights into your physical well-being and daily recovery without getting in your way. At just 8.2mm thin and sculpted from aerospace-grade recycled aluminum, it is designed for continuous 24/7 wear.

### Breakthrough Sensor Architecture
1. **Multi-Path PPG Array**: Features 5 dual-wavelength LEDs that adjust dynamically during high-intensity interval training (HIIT) to maintain continuous signal fidelity.
2. **Continuous EDA Stress Management**: Real-time electrodermal activity tracking detects acute bodily stress responses, prompting guided breathing exercises right on your wrist.
3. **Advanced Sleep Staging**: Combines heart rate variability (HRV), peripheral capillary oxygen saturation (SpO2), and skin temperature sensors to deliver clinical-grade sleep quality analyses.
