Operating rovers and exploratory robotic platforms on the surface of Mars or icy moons like Europa presents one fundamental challenge: speed-of-light communication latency. Commands sent from Earth can take anywhere from 4 to 24 minutes to reach the spacecraft.

To address this, Google has teamed up with NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) to develop **autonomous spatial traversal models** that let rovers evaluate terrain risks, avoid obstacles, and plot energy-efficient routes autonomously.

### High-Altitude Simulation and Testing
Using simulated lunar and martian surface topologies reconstructed from satellite telemetry, our joint teams have completed over 100,000 virtual exploration kilometers, achieving zero critical terrain stalls.
