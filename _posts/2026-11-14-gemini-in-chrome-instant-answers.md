Today we are rolling out a major update to Google Chrome that integrates **Gemini** directly into your everyday browsing workflow.

Whether you are reading a 50-page research paper, comparing hardware specifications on an e-commerce site, or debugging complex JavaScript code, Gemini in Chrome is right there in the side panel to assist you without switching tabs.

### What You Can Do
- **Instant Document Synthesis**: Summarize long technical whitepapers and articles with one click.
- **Multimodal Visual Grounding**: Ask questions about complex charts, architectural diagrams, and infographs rendered on any web page.
- **In-Context Code Debugging**: Highlight error traces in web consoles and get immediate root-cause explanations and suggested fixes.

Gemini in Chrome runs using a hybrid on-device and cloud architecture, ensuring snappy response times and enterprise-grade privacy protection.
