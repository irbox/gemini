Every year, billions of people turn to Google Search to find inspiration for gifts that bring joy to friends and family. 

Using Google Trends and Search data, we've analyzed the search patterns to bring you the **Holiday 100** — our annual compendium of the most sought-after products, gadgets, home goods, and wellness tech.

### Top Trends This Year
1. **Intelligent Wearables**: Health and fitness rings and smart glasses saw an unprecedented 180% surge in searches.
2. **Curated Kitchen Tech**: Smart appliances with automated temperature and induction precision topped home categories.
3. **Immersive Audio**: Spatial wireless earbuds with real-time multilingual translation capabilities.
