The latest Pixel Feature Drop begins rolling out to supported devices today, bringing intelligent updates to Pixel smartphones, Pixel Watch, and Pixel Tablet.

### What's New
- **Super Res Zoom with Generative Clarity**: Capture sharp, crisp details even at 30x digital zoom through local neural denoising.
- **Enhanced Call Assist**: Pixel Screen Call now features conversational voice synthesis that can ask clarifying questions and transcribe the conversation in real time.
- **Adaptive Battery 2.0**: Smart power management learns your daily application habits and dynamically tunes background processor states to extend battery life by up to 4 hours.
