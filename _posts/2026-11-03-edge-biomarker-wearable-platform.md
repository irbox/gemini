Continuous physiological sensing requires intimate skin contact, extreme microelectronic miniaturization, and mathematical filtering to reject motion artifacts.

The **Edge Biomarker Platform** introduces a 5-wavelength optical PPG sensor cluster combined with continuous electrodermal activity (cEDA) electrodes to monitor sympathetic nervous system tone throughout the day.

### Sensor Highlights
- **Active Motion Cancellation**: Dual-axis accelerometers feed accelerometer telemetry directly into an onboard Kalman filter that subtracts arm swing frequencies from arterial pulse curves.
- **Micro-Capillary SpO2 Monitoring**: Infrared and red dual-spectrum emitters measure capillary oxygen saturation across diverse skin tones with clinical precision.
- **All-Day Ergonomic Enclosure**: An ultra-thin 8.2mm curved aerospace-grade casing designed for continuous day-and-night comfort.
