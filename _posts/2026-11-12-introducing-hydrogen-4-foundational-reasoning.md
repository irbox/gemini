We are thrilled to unveil **Hydrogen-4**, our latest foundational model family designed explicitly for mathematical deduction, autonomous tool orchestration, and multi-step agentic planning.

Hydrogen-4 introduces a hybrid dense-sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) topology that activates only 12% of parameters per forward pass while maintaining access to a 1.2-trillion parameter emergent knowledge graph.

### Core Breakthroughs
- **Dynamic Reasoning Token Streams**: When presented with complex non-deterministic problems, Hydrogen-4 self-generates intermediate verification tokens, eliminating hallucinations in algebraic topology and compiler optimization benchmarks.
- **Fluid Audio-Visual Telemetry**: Hydrogen-4 perceives streaming 60fps video and stereophonic audio simultaneously with zero perceptible tokenization quantization lag.
- **Open Safety Benchmarks**: Exhaustively red-teamed across 40 evaluation categories with verifiable mathematical proofs of safety constraints.

Hydrogen-4 model weights and API endpoints are now available to academic researchers and open-source developers worldwide.
