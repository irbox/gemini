Today we are proud to introduce **Gemini 4 Hydrogen**, the latest leap forward in our foundational AI research and product development.

Built from the ground up by the teams across Google DeepMind and Google Research, Gemini 4 Hydrogen achieves record-setting benchmarks in multimodal comprehension, complex mathematical deduction, and zero-shot code synthesis.

### Key Highlights
- **Ultra-low latency reasoning**: Up to 3x faster response times with streaming neural synthesis.
- **Agentic Native Execution**: Capable of multi-step planning, tool orchestration, and self-verifying workflow execution.
- **Deep Multimodal Integration**: Fluid perception spanning high-definition video, audio, code, and spatial sensor telemetry.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen will begin rolling out across Google Workspace, Search, Android, and to developers via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI starting today.
