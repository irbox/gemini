Operating autonomous rovers on planetary bodies like Mars or Titan requires reckoning with the laws of physics: signals travelling at the speed of light take between 4 and 24 minutes each way to reach Earth.

To conquer this delay, our planetary robotics lab has deployed **autonomous spatial traversal models** that give robotic platforms complete autonomy to evaluate terrain hazards, avoid boulders, and plan optimal energy paths.

### High-Fidelity Simulation Validation
Using synthetic terrain scans reconstructed from orbital stereoscopic imagery, the platform completed over 120,000 simulated kilometers of continuous exploration, handling steep boulder fields, loose sand dunes, and sudden cliff hazards with zero human operator interventions.
