Our annual hardware retrospective examines data across 45 million device telemetry hours to assess which form factors are winning over real users in their daily routines.

### Key Trend Findings
1. **The 180% Surge in Discrete Wearables**: Smart rings and sensor-embedded glasses outpaced traditional wrist smartwatches for the first time, driven by 7-day battery life and seamless sleep tracking.
2. **Context-Aware Ambient Audio**: Earphones with onboard acoustic spatial awareness dynamically suppress ambient noise while highlighting conversation partners.
3. **Decentralized Personal Health Models**: Users overwhelmingly prefer devices that compute cardiac and metabolic biomarkers locally on edge silicon rather than uploading raw biosignals to third-party clouds.
